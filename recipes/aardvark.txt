Simon's Pork Sinigang (Tamarind Soup)
-----------------------------------
Time: 1 hour 30 minutes

Ingredients:
1 large onion, sliced
1 pack sinigang mix
2 lbs pork belly
2 pieces medium sized tomato, quartered
2 pieces taro
3 tbsp fish sauce
1/2 teaspoon salt
